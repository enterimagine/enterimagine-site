Privacy

In short

This website does not use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or visitor accounts. The only personal data we receive is what you choose to send with a What If? idea on the Collective Minds page. We do not sell or share any.

Who is responsible

The controller for this website is EnterImagine Media, Belgium. Contact: EnterImagine.media@enterimagine.com.

Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted on Microsoft Azure Static Web Apps. To deliver pages securely, the hosting provider automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your request. This is necessary to provide the site and protect it against abuse (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use this data to identify you.

Microsoft processes this data as our hosting provider. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies for visitors and stores nothing in your browser. Cookies may only be set by YouTube after you choose to load a video player (see below).

Sharing a What If? idea

On the Collective Minds page you can share an idea without creating an account. We store your idea text, the themes you choose, the display name you enter (optional), the time you sent it and when you gave consent. We do not ask for your email address and do not store your IP address.

Purpose and legal basis: to review your idea and, if approved, show it publicly with your display name on this website. We rely on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give with the checkbox on the form. Please do not include personal details in the idea itself.

Review: every idea is read by IMAGINE before it appears. Ideas that are not approved are deleted. Ideas not reviewed within 90 days are deleted automatically.

Withdrawing: after sending, you receive an idea number and a removal code. With them you can withdraw and delete your idea at any time on the Collective Minds page, or email EnterImagine.media@enterimagine.com with the idea number. Published ideas stay visible until you withdraw them or we remove them.

Spam protection: to limit misuse, we count submissions per visitor per day using a one-way code derived from your network address. The code cannot be turned back into the address and is deleted the next day.

Storage: ideas are stored in Microsoft Azure Table Storage in a data center in the European Union, operated by Microsoft as our processor. Moderation is done by IMAGINE only; the moderator signs in through GitHub, which sets a sign-in cookie for the moderator only, never for visitors.

Review assistance: to help review ideas and prepare replies from the founding minds, the idea text, the themes and the display name are sent to Anthropic (the Claude API) as our processor. Anthropic returns a suggestion and a draft reply; IMAGINE reads, edits and approves every reply before anything is published. No other data is sent, and nothing is published automatically.

Review alerts: when an idea arrives, the moderator receives a push notification through ntfy that only says how many ideas are waiting. It never contains your idea, name or any other data.

YouTube videos

Episode videos are not loaded automatically. Only when you choose “Load YouTube player” does your browser connect to YouTube (Google), using its privacy-enhanced mode. From that moment Google may process data and use cookies or similar storage. See the Google Privacy Policy. You can also watch each episode directly on YouTube.

Links to other websites

We link to scientific, public and company sources. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. Contact us at EnterImagine.media@enterimagine.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

Changes

If we add new features that use personal data, this notice will be updated before they go live. Last updated: 29 October 2026.
