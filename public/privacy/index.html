Privacy

In short

This website does not use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, accounts or forms. We do not ask you for personal data, and we do not sell or share any.

Who is responsible

The controller for this website is EnterImagine Media, Belgium. Contact: EnterImagine.media@gmail.com.

Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted on Microsoft Azure Static Web Apps. To deliver pages securely, the hosting provider automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your request. This is necessary to provide the site and protect it against abuse (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use this data to identify you.

Microsoft processes this data as our hosting provider. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Cookies may only be set by YouTube after you choose to load a video player (see below).

YouTube videos

Episode videos are not loaded automatically. Only when you choose “Load YouTube player” does your browser connect to YouTube (Google), using its privacy-enhanced mode. From that moment Google may process data and use cookies or similar storage. See the Google Privacy Policy. You can also watch each episode directly on YouTube.

Links to other websites

We link to scientific, public and company sources. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. Contact us at EnterImagine.media@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

Changes

If this website starts to collect personal data, for example through future community features, this notice will be updated before that happens. Last updated: 28 October 2026.
