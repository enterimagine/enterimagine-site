Explore with Dali

The idea, simply

A different way to see what is possible

Energy systems bring together generation, storage, networks and the ways we use power. Cleaner energy is a systems challenge: electricity needs to reach people reliably, at an affordable cost, with fewer environmental impacts.

Current reality

Where we are today

Renewable generation, batteries and grid monitoring are already in use. Infrastructure, local conditions and supply chains shape what can be deployed. Reliability still requires balancing supply and demand.

Future possibility

What could come next

Better storage, flexible demand and coordinated grids could help integrate more clean electricity. Each approach needs testing for cost, safety, durability and resilience.

Human outcomes

Why it matters

Reliable energy supports homes, healthcare and livelihoods. A cleaner transition should also consider affordability, land use, materials and communities.

  • More resilient local electricity systems
  • Storage that helps balance changing supply and demand
  • Cleaner power with attention to affordability
