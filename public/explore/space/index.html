The idea, simply

A different way to see what is possible

Space technology includes satellites, instruments, launch systems and exploration. Looking outward also helps us understand Earth: observations connect distant measurements with decisions about our own planet.

Current reality

Where we are today

Satellites support Earth observation, communications and navigation. Scientific missions expand what we know, while launches and orbital operations bring costs, debris and environmental concerns.

Future possibility

What could come next

New instruments and more capable off-world systems could extend exploration. Long-duration activity beyond Earth remains a demanding engineering challenge, with uncertain costs and timelines.

Human outcomes

Why it matters

Space can deepen our understanding of climate, resources and our place in the universe. Useful exploration should also consider responsible orbital use and benefits on Earth.

  • Earth observations that support informed decisions
  • Scientific exploration of unfamiliar environments
  • More resilient systems for demanding conditions
