Connecting people and places with more thoughtful movement.

Explore with Dali

The idea, simply

A different way to see what is possible

Smart mobility connects transport choices, vehicles and infrastructure. It includes public transport, electric vehicles, useful travel information and carefully tested automation. The goal is easier movement for people, not simply more technology on the road.

Current reality

Where we are today

Electric vehicles and driver-assistance features are available. Assistance still requires drivers to remain responsible; capabilities vary by system and conditions. Charging access and safe infrastructure remain practical challenges.

Future possibility

What could come next

Better coordination of transport, charging and routing could reduce friction. More automated services may become useful in selected settings, but safety and dependable operation must be demonstrated.

Human outcomes

Why it matters

Transport shapes access to work, healthcare and community. Improvements should serve people with different abilities and budgets while considering congestion, emissions and public space.

  • More accessible journeys across transport modes
  • Charging coordinated with local energy systems
  • Safer infrastructure and carefully evaluated automation
