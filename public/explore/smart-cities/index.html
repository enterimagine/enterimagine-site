Smart Cities

Technology working together for more livable places.

Explore with Dali

The idea, simply

A different way to see what is possible

A smart city uses information and connected infrastructure to improve urban life. Its intelligence lies in how well it serves people: buildings, transport, water, energy and public services need to work as a coherent system.

Current reality

Where we are today

Cities use digital tools to monitor infrastructure and plan services. Data quality, fragmented systems, budgets and public accountability limit what those tools can achieve.

Future possibility

What could come next

Better coordination could help cities adapt to heat, disruption and changing needs. Digital models and connected services need real-world validation, privacy protections and meaningful community involvement.

Human outcomes

Why it matters

Livability means accessible services, healthy public spaces and infrastructure people can rely on. Technology should support those goals while avoiding exclusion and unnecessary surveillance.

  • More resilient neighbourhood infrastructure
  • Services planned around residents’ needs
  • Better coordination of energy and mobility

When Parking Goes Underground

Surface parking takes up some of the most valuable land in our cities. Episode 004 imagines a glass pavilion on a city square: you drive in, step out, and the whole bay sinks below the street. Underground, a loop of ten self-moving bays keeps charging and brings your car back up, already facing the exit.
