Connections

The intersections may matter most

Technology rarely advances in isolation. Some of the most important breakthroughs may emerge when different fields begin to reinforce one another.

These connections have different levels of readiness. A deployed system, a research direction and an imagined outcome are different starting points.

How to read the universe

From current reality to future possibility

These are editorial descriptions of a specific capability or scenario, not scores, predictions or investment ratings. Read the scope and limitations alongside each label.

Established
Technology already widely deployed or operational.
Emerging
Real systems and deployments exist, but capability or adoption is still developing.
Experimental
Active research and prototypes exist, but practical utility remains uncertain or limited.
Imagined
A speculative future scenario used by EnterImagine to explore what could become possible.
