The perspective

A mind for possibility

Explores how intelligence, data and human creativity can work together to expand what is possible.

Sofia sees intelligence as a meeting point between data, human judgement and creative possibility. She asks how AI could help us notice patterns, explore ideas and make better-informed decisions.

Her perspective keeps people at the centre: what should we delegate, what should we question, and what becomes possible when human creativity works alongside digital intelligence?
