What happens when technologies stop evolving separately and start working as one system?

The perspective

A mind for connected systems

Connects technology, infrastructure and human needs to see the bigger picture.

Dali looks at the connections between transport, energy and the places we call home. A better vehicle matters, but so do the electricity that powers it, the streets it travels and the people who need access to it.

He brings an optimistic systems perspective: bring disciplines together, listen to communities and test how an improvement works across the whole network. Progress is strongest when its benefits reach everyday life.

Current reality

Where we are today

Cities and energy networks already connect many technologies. Making them work together depends on standards, maintenance, investment and public trust.

Future possibility

What could come next

More coordinated infrastructure could help communities use resources wisely and adapt to change. Integration needs practical trials and clear responsibility, not just more sensors.
